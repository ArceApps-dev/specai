Enter user feedback iteration loop:

1. Ask what isn't working or needs to change.
2. Update living documents (_plan.md, _tasks.md).
3. Add corrective tasks under ## Iteration Tasks.
4. Execute corrective tasks.
5. Re-verify after fixes.

Begin by asking what issues were found.
